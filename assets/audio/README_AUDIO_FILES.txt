GAMEPLAY SOUND EFFECTS
======================

Background music and continuous ambience loops are intentionally omitted.
All thirteen existing gameplay SFX are retained without byte changes:

weapon/: punch-whoosh.wav, punch-impact.wav
skill/: fireball.mp3, ice-pike.wav, poison.wav, poison-tick.wav
action/: guard.wav
system/: tab-sfx.wav, confirm-sfx.wav, encounter-sfx.wav,
         victory-sfx.wav, defeat-sfx.wav, typewriter-sfx.wav

AudioManager maps these files to attacks, skills, hits, guard, UI navigation,
dialogue typing, encounters and battle results. The fireball MP3 is a gameplay
effect, so it remains. Source and redistribution rights need owner verification;
see docs/THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md.
